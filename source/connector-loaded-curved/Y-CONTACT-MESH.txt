build-y-crimp-mesh.py rebuilds the original analytic terminal and generates
loaded-y-crimp-frame.js. Run with --output pointing to an output directory.
The nominal terminal BREP, circular bore radii, sheet features and wire stock
are retained. The display mesh uses a circumscribed bore boundary instead of
an inscribed chord, and retains the complete frame and shared cap boundaries.
The Y runtime includes a material sample at its actual straight-entry join.

The generated helper validates the original source tuples and shares one
template clone per immutable frame. After the actual local-origin transform
and Float32 write, it checks the bore's encoded side planes using outward
IEEE754 intervals. If necessary, shared bore vertices move only to successive
outward Float32 values until those planes enclose the unchanged nominal circle
and all actual straight-entry wire triangles. The nominal BREP and wire gauge
are not enlarged or shrunk. runtime/y-crimp-rounding.js is the original source
template appended by the producer. It is not a separate runtime dependency.

This is an independently authored teaching reference. Published family facts
do not identify an OEM terminal SKU, finished crimp dimensions, production fit,
material force or electrical rating. Numerical triangle contact observations
are separate from those physical properties.
