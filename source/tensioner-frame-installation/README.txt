Editable preferred sources are dist/tensioner-installation-model.js, dist/tensioner-installation-plan.js and dist/tensioner-installation-source.js. Existing whole native CAD parts, the complete three-solid GT2-20 idlers and the complete nominal2.5mm L-key from original-tensioner-model.js remain the geometry sources. No new stock geometry is generated here.

The assembled left9/right8 workpiece members move rigidly to the retained source mounting datums. Five distinct native M3x30 screws and one native M3x18 screw then fasten the parts. One supplied Allen key is represented by mutually exclusive workpiece poses. For the left M3x18, outward half-turn strokes, complete withdrawal and repositioning avoid the adjacent taller part; the key and part are not shortened or cut.

Nominal0.5mm screw pitch, final2/2.5mm advancement and the complete80/20mm-arm R3 L-key are teaching kinematics and reference dimensions. They do not specify OEM torque, force, elastic response, manufacturing tolerances or independently certified fit. Existing source/library mating contacts remain nonzero. Original and Plus retain their own official step, line and photo references. This correspondence does not grant Gen2 hardware equivalence.
